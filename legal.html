KHTE Gas Services provides plumbing, heat and air-conditioning installation services provided by KHTE GAS SERVICES LTD, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 12436971. IMPORTANT — GAS SAFE REGISTRATION AND APPLIANCE CATEGORIES: under the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998 work on a gas fitting or gas appliance may be carried out only by a competent person who is a member of a class of persons approved for the purpose, in practice registration with the Gas Safe Register, and carrying out gas work without that registration is a criminal offence. REGISTRATION ALONE IS NOT SUFFICIENT: the reverse of a Gas Safe identification card lists the specific categories of gas work and appliance for which the individual engineer is qualified, and AN ENGINEER MAY BE REGISTERED YET NOT QUALIFIED FOR A PARTICULAR APPLIANCE TYPE, including commercial appliances, gas fires, cookers, warm air units or liquefied petroleum gas. The company's registration is held under [GAS SAFE REGISTRATION NUMBER] and the categories held are [APPLIANCE CATEGORIES HELD]; THE IDENTIFICATION CARD IS PRODUCED BEFORE WORK BEGINS AND CLIENTS ARE ENCOURAGED TO READ THE REVERSE AND TO VERIFY REGISTRATION WITH THE GAS SAFE REGISTER. The company does not undertake gas work outside the categories for which it is qualified and refers such work to an appropriately qualified engineer. UNSAFE APPLIANCES: where an appliance or installation is found to be immediately dangerous or at risk, the company follows the industry unsafe situations procedure, which may involve disconnecting or turning off and capping the appliance with the client's permission, affixing a warning notice, and where permission is refused in the case of an immediately dangerous situation reporting the matter as required; THE COMPANY WILL NOT LEAVE AN APPLIANCE IT HAS IDENTIFIED AS IMMEDIATELY DANGEROUS IN USE, whatever the occupier's preference. Suspected carbon monoxide exposure is treated as an emergency. LANDLORDS: a landlord must ensure that gas appliances and flues in relevant premises are checked for safety at intervals of not more than twelve months by a registered engineer, must give a copy of the record to each existing tenant within twenty-eight days of the check and to a new tenant at the start of the tenancy, and must retain records for two years. In England, rented premises require a carbon monoxide alarm in any room used as living accommodation containing a fixed combustion appliance other than a gas cooker. The company issues records accordingly, but THE STATUTORY DUTY REMAINS THE LANDLORD'S. BUILDING REGULATIONS: installation and flueing of combustion appliances is carried out in accordance with the applicable Building Regulations requirements, appliances are commissioned and benchmarked where applicable, and notifiable work is notified. NO APPLIANCE LIFESPAN OR CONTINUED OPERATION IS GUARANTEED beyond the manufacturer's warranty and any workmanship warranty stated in writing; an appliance's condition depends on age, servicing history, water quality and use.
